## Purpose

Welcome new customers within two business days of Closed Won and set the success plan.

## Scope

- **In scope:** Welcome email, kickoff scheduling, and success plan draft.
- **Out of scope:** Deep training delivery (SOP-CS-002).

## Preconditions

- [ ] Closed Won / onboarding started
- [ ] Named CS owner

## Required Access

| System / Console | Permission / Role | Notes |
|------------------|-------------------|-------|
| CRM / email | CS | Send welcome |

## Step-by-step Procedure

### 1. Welcome within 2 business days

- [ ] Welcome email with Knowledge Center and support channel
- [ ] Kickoff scheduled
- [ ] Success plan draft (goals from discovery)

See [Welcome](../../customer-success/welcome.md).

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] Welcome sent
- [ ] Kickoff booked
- [ ] Success plan draft attached
- [ ] CS owner visible on account
- [ ] Evidence attached to the controlling ticket / change record

## Rollback Procedure

If delayed, escalate to CS Lead and send interim welcome same day.
