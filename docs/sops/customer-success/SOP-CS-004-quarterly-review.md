## Purpose

Run a QBR covering outcomes, usage, roadmap asks, renewal risk, and expansion.

## Scope

- **In scope:** QBR prep, meeting, and follow-ups.
- **Out of scope:** Contract renewal commercial execution (Billing/SOP-CS-005).

## Preconditions

- [ ] Health history available
- [ ] Executive sponsor identified when possible

## Required Access

| System / Console | Permission / Role | Notes |
|------------------|-------------------|-------|
| QBR deck / CRM | CS | Facilitate QBR |

## Step-by-step Procedure

### 1. Deliver QBR

Use [QBR template](../../customer-success/qbr.md): outcomes, usage, roadmap ask, renewal risk, expansion.
Capture actions with owners and dates.

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] QBR held or formally deferred
- [ ] Actions logged
- [ ] Renewal risk updated
- [ ] Expansion signals handed to AE if any
- [ ] Evidence attached to the controlling ticket / change record

## Rollback Procedure

If QBR cancelled by customer, send written summary and reschedule within 30 days.

## Exceptions

| Exception | Handling | Approver |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| None documented | Follow change-management exception path | Operations Lead |
