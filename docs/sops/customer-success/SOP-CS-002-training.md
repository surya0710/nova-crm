## Purpose

Deliver enablement sessions so admins and users can operate scoped modules.

## Scope

- **In scope:** Training schedule, session delivery, and attendance tracking.
- **Out of scope:** Product feature building and demo engineering.

## Preconditions

- [ ] Welcome complete or kickoff done
- [ ] Module scope known

## Required Access

| System / Console | Permission / Role | Notes |

| Meeting / LMS | CS | Deliver training |

### 1. Schedule

| Week | Session |

| 1 | Core workspace (CRM / Projects / HR as scoped) |
| 2 | Reporting & adoption clinic |
| 4 | Office hours |
