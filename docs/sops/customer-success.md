## Purpose
Drive long-term value: welcome, train, measure health, renew, expand, and prevent churn.

Playbooks: [Customer Success](../customer-success/README.md)

## 1. Welcome process
Within 2 business days of Closed Won:
- [ ] Welcome email with Knowledge Center and support channel
- [ ] Kickoff scheduled
- [ ] Success plan draft (goals from discovery)

## 2. Training schedule
| Week | Session |
|------|---------|
| 0 | Admin essentials |
| 1 | Core workspace (CRM / Projects / HR as scoped) |
| 2 | Reporting & adoption clinic |
| 4 | Office hours |

## 3. Health checks
Monthly (bi-weekly for pilots): login ratio, module adoption, open P1/P2, data hygiene.  
Template: [Health checks](../customer-success/health-checks.md).

## 4. Adoption review
Day 30 / 60 / 90 vs success plan; assign enablement for gaps.

## 5. Quarterly business review
Outcomes, usage, roadmap ask, renewal risk, expansion. Template: [QBR](../customer-success/qbr.md).

## 6. Renewal workflow
T-90 / T-60 / T-30 in [Renewal](../customer-success/renewal.md).

## 7. Upsell process
Identify expansion (seats, modules, premium support). Hand to AE with usage evidence — CS does not discount.

## 8. Customer feedback collection
NPS / CSAT after go-live and quarterly; route themes to Product.

## 9. Churn prevention
Red flags: declining logins, unpaid invoices, unresolved P2s, champion departure. Escalate within 48 hours.  
[Churn prevention](../customer-success/churn-prevention.md).

## Exit criteria
Named CS owner, health score current, renewal date tracked.
