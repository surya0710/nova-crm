# CRM User Guide - Leads

## Purpose
Manage lead capture, qualification, and follow-up actions.

## Who should use this feature
Sales representatives, sales coordinators, and managers.

## Prerequisites
- CRM access permissions
- Lead source and ownership rules configured

## Step-by-step instructions
1. Create a lead with complete profile details.
2. Assign owner and set next follow-up date.
3. Update qualification status after interaction.
4. Convert qualified lead when criteria are met.

## Expected result
Leads move through a consistent and trackable lifecycle.

## Best Practices
Use standardized qualification criteria and timely updates.

## Common Mistakes
Missing mandatory details and skipped follow-up tasks.

## FAQ
Document recurring questions about lead conversion and ownership.
