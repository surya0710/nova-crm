# CRM User Guide - Customers

## Purpose
Maintain customer records and account context.

## Who should use this feature
Sales teams, account managers, and support operations.

## Prerequisites
- Valid customer data standards
- Access to customer management actions

## Step-by-step instructions
1. Create customer profile with required identifiers.
2. Link contacts, opportunities, and billing details.
3. Update status and ownership when account changes.
4. Review account timeline before major actions.

## Expected result
Accurate customer records support sales and service workflows.

## Best Practices
Keep customer ownership and contact details current.

## Common Mistakes
Duplicate records and incomplete contact mapping.

## FAQ
Capture rules for merges, ownership transfer, and deactivation.
