# CRM User Guide - Payments

## Purpose
Record and reconcile incoming customer payments.

## Who should use this feature
Finance teams and authorized billing operators.

## Prerequisites
- Published invoice exists
- Payment method and account mappings configured

## Step-by-step instructions
1. Open target invoice and start payment entry.
2. Enter amount, method, reference, and date.
3. Save and confirm allocation status.
4. Reconcile partial/over payments as needed.

## Expected result
Payment records are accurate and invoice status is updated.

## Best Practices
Capture payment references and reconcile daily.

## Common Mistakes
Wrong invoice allocation and missing reference metadata.

## FAQ
Document split payments, reversals, and adjustment handling.
