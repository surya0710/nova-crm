# CRM Overview

## Purpose
Introduces CRM capabilities, scope, and key user journeys.

## Core Areas
- Lead and customer lifecycle
- Contacts under each company
- Company relationship hub (opportunities, quotes, orders, invoices, payments, tickets, activities)
- Opportunity, quotation, sales order, invoice, and payment flow
- Receivables, aging, and customer ledger
- Credit and debit notes (historical invoice totals stay unchanged)
- Price lists and customer-specific pricing
- Customer portal billing
- Sales and revenue dashboard widgets
- Invoicing and payment visibility
- Operational reporting, including CRM email delivery metrics
- Commercial REST APIs, RBAC, and tenant isolation
- Organization email (composer, templates, conversations, workflow send)
