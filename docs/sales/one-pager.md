# NovaCRM — One-page Product Summary

**NovaCRM** unifies **CRM**, **Projects**, **HRMS**, **Marketing**, and **Analytics** in one multi-tenant SaaS platform.

## Outcomes
- Shorten quote-to-cash
- Plan and deliver work with visibility
- Run people operations without a separate stack
- Attribute marketing to revenue
- Give leaders one analytics surface

## Ideal customer
Mid-market teams (≈25–500 staff) consolidating tools across sales, delivery, and HR.
