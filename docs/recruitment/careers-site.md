# Careers Site

## Purpose
Each organization can operate a public careers site at `{organization-slug}/careers` with branding, job listings, search, and filters.

## CMS Settings
Organization admins with `recruitment.careers.manage` can configure:
- Logo and banner
- About us, benefits, culture, mission
- Social links and recruitment contact details
- SEO metadata and custom footer
- Publish/unpublish state

## Public Pages
- Careers home with open positions and filters
- Job detail with responsibilities, requirements, benefits, and apply actions

## Service
`CareerSiteService` owns landing page queries, CMS updates, and published opening resolution.
