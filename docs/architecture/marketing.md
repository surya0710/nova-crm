# Marketing Architecture

## Purpose
Technical structure for marketing domain capabilities.

## Diagram
Marketing UI -> Controllers -> Campaign Services -> Data Store

## Database Tables
Campaigns, audiences, engagements, and attribution entities.

## Services
Segmentation, campaign orchestration, and analytics aggregation.

## Controllers
Endpoints for campaign lifecycle and reporting operations.

## Policies
Role-based controls for campaign creation and approvals.

## Workflow Events
Campaign created, launched, paused, completed.

## Notifications
Approval prompts, delivery status, and anomaly alerts.

## Audit
Track content edits, targeting changes, and approvals.

## RBAC
Define marketer, approver, and analyst capabilities.

## Extension Points
Channel connectors and campaign rule processors.

## Future Improvements
Personalization and multi-channel optimization roadmap.
