## Workspaces at a glance

- **Home** — personal work and widgets
- **CRM** — leads, customers, pipeline, quotations, invoices
- **Projects** — projects, tasks, portfolios, resources
- **HR** — people, time, leave, recruitment, payroll foundations
- **Marketing** — campaigns and attribution
- **Analytics** — executive and module KPIs
- **Administration** — org settings (admins)

Use the command palette (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`) or Knowledge Center (`/knowledge`) to jump quickly.
