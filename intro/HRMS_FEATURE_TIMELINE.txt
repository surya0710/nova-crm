--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visuals: Employee directory → profile → documents → departments → designations
         → branches → shifts → holidays → leave types → org settings hub
Narration:
  "HR starts from a complete people foundation — identity, org structure,
   shifts, holidays, and document records in one tenant-scoped workspace."

================================================================================
01:40–02:30  SCENE 3 — EMPLOYEE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visuals: Create/edit employee surfaces, assignment fields, status, linkage
Narration:
  "HR maintains the employee master record — department, designation, branch,
   shift, status, and portal linkage — without leaving the HRMS workspace."

================================================================================
02:30–03:30  SCENE 4 — ATTENDANCE LIFECYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visuals: Attendance dashboard → summary → reports → calendar → corrections
         → attendance record detail
Narration:
  "Attendance follows a clear lifecycle: employee check-in, verification,
   working day tracking, check-out, and durable attendance records with
   history and reporting."

================================================================================
03:30–04:20  SCENE 5 — GEO-ATTENDANCE & VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visuals: Attendance rules (verification mode) → geofences → record metadata
Narration:
  "Organization policy controls verification. Branch geofences can enforce
   location, verification evidence is retained, and existing attendance
   versioning remains intact. Biometric proof foundations are available when
   policy requires them — without claiming unconfigured hardware vendors."

================================================================================
04:20–05:30  SCENE 6 — WORK FROM HOME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visuals: WFH policies → permanent assignments → requests → detail → approvals
Narration:
  "WFH supports permanent assignments, daily and multi-day requests, manager
   and HR approval, cancellation, and attendance interaction.
   Office days enforce geofence; approved WFH can exempt geofence while
   biometric policy can still apply; approved leave takes precedence over WFH."

================================================================================
05:30–06:20  SCENE 7 — LEAVE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visuals: Leave dashboard → types → balances → applications → approval queue
Narration:
  "Leave covers configuration, balances, employee requests, manager or HR
   approval, history, and interaction with attendance and WFH conflict rules."

================================================================================
06:20–07:20  SCENE 8 — PAYROLL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Visuals: Payroll home → configuration → components → structures → assignments
         → periods → runs → payslips → reports
Narration:
  "Payroll connects salary configuration, calculation surfaces, attendance and
   leave-aware processing, statutory handling, publication, and payslip output
   as available in the current build."

================================================================================
07:20–07:50  SCENE 9 — TAX / TDS

Visuals: Tax workspace → regimes/declarations → statutory
Narration:
  "Tax and TDS configuration, employee declarations, and statutory workflows
